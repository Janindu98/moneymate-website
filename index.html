Application Purpose & Design Philosophy

What is the Purpose of MoneyMate Vault?

MoneyMate Vault is a desktop personal finance application engineered to give you complete ownership and clarity over your personal finances.

Designed for Comprehensive Personal Financial Management on Your Windows PC

The primary purpose of MoneyMate Vault is to empower individuals, professionals, and households with a single, unified, and secure desktop solution to organize daily income and expenses, maintain multiple bank accounts, track salary histories and payslips, and generate actionable financial reports without compromising privacy.

Unlike cloud-only accounting tools that store your sensitive financial transactions on external servers or require monthly recurring subscriptions, MoneyMate Vault adopts an offline-first desktop architecture. All your financial ledgers and records remain strictly on your local computer in a secure SQLite database protected with client-side encryption.

Google Drive Integration & Cloud Backup

Secure Cloud Backup & Data Transparency

MoneyMate Vault can create encrypted backups of your financial data and store those encrypted backup files in the user's Google Drive. Financial data is encrypted before it is uploaded.

You maintain full sovereignty over your financial data. Backups are stored in your own Google Drive storage account in an AES-256 encrypted format (.enc) that cannot be read by anyone without your personal encryption password.

Why MoneyMate Vault Requests Google Drive Access

OAuth Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

MoneyMate Vault requests access to Google Drive only when you choose to use the Google Drive backup feature. This access allows MoneyMate Vault to upload and retrieve your encrypted .enc backup files from your Google Drive. Your financial data is encrypted locally on your computer before the backup is uploaded. MoneyMate Vault does not use Google Drive access to access or manage unrelated files in your Google Drive.

1. Purpose of Data Access

Access is requested strictly to save, update, and restore your client-side encrypted backup packages (.enc) to your own Google Drive storage.

2. Limited Per-File Scope (drive.file)

MoneyMate Vault only accesses files created by the application itself. It cannot view, read, edit, or delete any other personal documents, spreadsheets, or photos in your Google Drive.

3. Client-Side Encryption Guarantee

Financial ledger records are encrypted with AES-256 on your local device before upload. Neither Google nor MoneyMate Vault developers can read your plain financial data.

4. Local Token Storage & Revocation

OAuth tokens remain on your local computer. You can revoke access at any time via Google Account Permissions or in-app settings.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure: MoneyMate Vault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, AI model training, or sold to third-party brokers.
