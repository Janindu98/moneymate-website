Google Drive Integration

Secure Cloud Backup

MoneyMate Vault can create encrypted backups of your financial data and store those encrypted backup files in the user's Google Drive. Financial data is encrypted before it is uploaded.

You maintain full sovereignty over your data. Backups are stored in your own Google Drive storage account in a protected format that cannot be read by anyone without your encryption password.

How Encrypted Backup Works
1
Local AES Encryption

Your financial ledger is encrypted on your computer before leaving the device.

2
Direct Google Drive Upload

The encrypted .enc backup file is transferred directly to your Google Drive.

3
Zero-Knowledge Storage

No unencrypted data is ever accessible to third parties or remote servers.

Google OAuth 2.0 & Data Access Transparency
Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

To ensure complete transparency regarding Google Account data access during Google OAuth verification:

1. Purpose of Data Access

Access is requested strictly to save, update, and restore your client-side encrypted backup packages (.enc) to your own Google Drive storage.

2. Limited Per-File Scope

MoneyMate Vault only accesses files created by the application itself. It cannot view, read, edit, or delete any other personal documents, spreadsheets, or photos in your Google Drive.

3. Client-Side Encryption

Financial ledger records are encrypted with AES-256 on your local device before upload. Neither Google nor MoneyMate developers can read your plain financial data.

4. Local Token Storage & Revocation

OAuth tokens remain on your local computer. You can revoke access at any time via Google Account Permissions or in-app settings.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure: MoneyMate Vault's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, AI model training, or sold to third-party brokers.
Verified Application Capabilities

Features

Everything you need to manage your personal finances and salary records with total security and zero tracking.

Income & Expense Tracking

Record and categorize daily expenses, monitor incoming cash flow, and maintain clear, accurate ledgers of all personal transactions.

Bank Account Management

Organize multiple bank accounts, savings deposits, cash wallets, and cards in one unified dashboard with instant balance tracking.

Salary Records

Log employment earnings, manage payslips, track salary breakdowns, deductions, and statutory allowances securely in your local database.

Financial Reports

Generate insightful reports, visual spending breakdowns, and comprehensive financial summaries to analyze your budget trends over time.

Encrypted Local Backups

Export complete, password-protected encrypted .enc archive packages directly to your PC or external drives whenever you wish.

Secure Google Drive Backups

Upload encrypted backup files directly to your personal Google Drive for protected cloud redundancy. Financial data is encrypted before it is uploaded.

Offline-First Operation

Runs entirely locally on your Windows PC. No remote servers, third-party accounts, or active internet connection required to manage your finances.

Your Privacy is Guaranteed

No Developer Server or Remote Financial Database

MoneyMate Vault does not transmit your financial transactions, salary figures, bank balances, or personal notes to a MoneyMate developer server or remote financial database.

When you explicitly enable cloud backup, MoneyMate encrypts your backup locally before uploading the encrypted backup file to your selected cloud storage provider, such as Google Drive.
